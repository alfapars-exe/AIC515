\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
\subsection{Derived ground truths}
From every verified pair we derive, besides the official \GTb{} mask $M^{b}$: \GTp{}, the pixel-change mask $\{D>8\}$ cleaned by a $3{\times}3$ opening, removal of components below 8\,px and a $3{\times}3$ closing; and \GTg{}, Sauvola text pixels~\cite{sauvola2000binarization} of $I^f\cup I^a$ intersected with a $7{\times}7$ dilation of $\{D>8\}$, with polarity inverted on dark backgrounds. Median IoU with the box is 0.35 (\GTp{}) and 0.27 (\GTg{}). Because the marker line differs from the authentic image, it is part of \GTp{} (median 10\,\% of its positives in affected pairs); the variants \GTp$_{\mathrm{nm}}$ and \GTg$_{\mathrm{nm}}$ remove the dilated residue pixels.

\subsection{Controls and probes}
On the validation pairs we build: \emph{benign paste-back} (box region of the authentic image up/down-sampled $2\times$ and pasted back, i.e.\ compositing without content change) and \emph{full-image resampling}~\cite{mareen2024tgif}. Input-level probes (Fig.~\ref{fig:probes}) are: \emph{forged-repaired} ($I^f$ with residue pixels copied from $I^a$); \emph{authentic+marker} ($I^a$ with only the residue pixels from $I^f$); \emph{render-only} ($I^f$ with the dilated \GTp{} region and the residue reverted to $I^a$, so no character changes but the generator's sub-threshold re-rendering of the field remains); \emph{content-only} ($I^a$ with only the dilated \GTp$_{\mathrm{nm}}$ region taken from $I^f$, so characters change but the surrounding re-rendering and the marker are absent); a boundary/placebo/interior blur ablation of the box; and test-time degradations (JPEG Q75, $0.75\times$ rescale, Gaussian $\sigma{=}1$, CLAHE, grayscale).

\subsection{Models}
\textbf{Segmenters.} A U-Net~\cite{ronneberger2015unet} with an ImageNet-initialised ResNet-18 encoder~\cite{he2016resnet} (14.3\,M parameters) is trained on $512{\times}512$ native-resolution crops (70\,\% centred on training-mask positives with jitter, 30\,\% random), BCE+Dice loss~\cite{milletari2016vnet}, AdamW~\cite{loshchilov2019adamw} ($10^{-4}$, cosine), batch 16, bf16, 10 epochs, model selection by validation AUPRC under the \emph{training} mask. Inference is full-image sliding window (512, stride 448); the 44 images whose longer side exceeds 4096\,px are downscaled to 4096 for inference and the map is upsampled back. The training mask is the only factor varied (\GTb, \GTp, \GTg; seeds 42--44). One-factor extensions, seed 42: (i) DeepLabV3+~\cite{chen2018deeplabv3plus} with the same encoder; (ii) training on marker-repaired images with \GTb, \GTp$_{\mathrm{nm}}$, \GTg$_{\mathrm{nm}}$; (iii) a joint model with a second output channel supervised by \GTb{} (loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{pix}}+0.5\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{box}}$, evaluation on the pixel channel); (iv) 20 epochs; (v) Gemini-only training for the held-out-generator test (seeds 42--44).

\textbf{Box detector.} YOLO11~\cite{jocher2024yolo11} (single class) is trained on the same \GTb{} boxes. Because the median field is 33\,px high, images are cut at native resolution into $1024{\times}1024$ tiles (stride 768; every tile holding $\ge50\,\%$ of the box is labelled with the clipped box, and one other random tile per image is kept with an empty label, which can contain a sliver of the field) and inference is tiled with cross-tile NMS, following slicing-aided detection~\cite{akyon2022sahi}. Augmentation is conservative (no mosaic or scale jitter, which would add resampling traces); AdamW $10^{-3}$, batch 8, 30 epochs, patience 10, checkpoint selection by Ultralytics' default fitness ($0.1\,$mAP@0.5$\,+\,0.9\,$mAP@[.5:.95]) on validation tiles. Boxes are taken from the released metadata, which agrees with the mask extent except for 12 images (0.3\,\%). One-factor ablations (seed 42): whole image resized to 1280; default Ultralytics augmentation; one extra empty authentic tile per image; model size n/s/m; Gemini-only training. For pixel metrics, each box paints its confidence into a map (maximum over overlapping boxes), so that detector and segmenters pass through \emph{identical} evaluation code.

\subsection{Evaluation and statistics}
Every model is scored against \GTb, \GTp{} and \GTg{} on the 487 validation images and against \GTb{} on the 812 test images (\GTp{} where a test pair exists, $n{=}89$). Metrics are pooled pixel AUPRC (exact, from 256-bin histograms over all pixels), per-image AP, F1/IoU at a threshold $\tau^*$ calibrated per model and ground truth on validation, and, for all models, box-level AP@0.5 and AP@[.5:.95] (COCO-style~\cite{lin2014coco}; segmenter boxes from connected components at $\tau^*$, scored by their maximum probability). Image-level discrimination compares forged with authentic validation images using the 99th-percentile score (benign controls) or the maximum score (input probes, Tables~\ref{tab:marker} and~\ref{tab:render}). Uncertainty is a 1{,}000-sample bootstrap over source-document groups~\cite{efron1979bootstrap}; contrasts within a hypothesis family are Holm-corrected~\cite{holm1979}. Four hypotheses and their falsification criteria were fixed before the full-data runs: H1, the training ground truth changes scores and model ranking; H2, the box model relies on the box boundary or rectangle shape; H3, pixel or glyph supervision transfers better to an unseen generator and degradations; H4, forgeries carry a global trace outside the box; the marker, render/content, YOLO and joint-loss analyses were added afterwards and are reported as exploratory.
