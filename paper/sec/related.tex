\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Document and text tampering localization}
Most document forensics work treats localization as dense prediction. DocTamper~\cite{qu2023doctamper} paired a large synthetic corpus with a network that reads JPEG block-DCT statistics, and later localizers refine how frequency and RGB cues are fused or made robust to recompression~\cite{chen2024ffdn,wong2025adcdnet,dhouib2026diffnet,docqt2026,ctpnet2023,song2024caftbnet,dong2024tce,li2025ditl2}. A parallel line, originating in scene text, scores whole text instances instead of pixels: Tampered-IC13 adapts scene-text detectors so that each detected instance is also judged genuine or tampered~\cite{wang2022tamperedic13,qu2025ostf}. Text-centric benchmarks for receipts and documents, including T-SROIE, the ICDAR 2023 competition, RTM and a recent human-crafted real-world set, broaden the data, with labels that range from instance boxes to pixel masks~\cite{wang2022tsroie,luo2023icdar,luo2025rtm,xu2026realitygap}. Newer systems couple localization with explanation through multimodal language models or generative restoration~\cite{qu2024textsleuth,qu2026textshieldr1,zeng2026docshield,zhang2026scrf,gentextforensics2026}. For API-inpainted financial documents, AIForge-Doc v1 and v2~\cite{wu2026aiforgedoc,wu2026aiforgedocv2} and related studies~\cite{zhao2026docforgebench,zhang2026gpt4oreceipt,mllmdoc2025,textfake2026,raj2026images25} report detectors in a zero-shot setting only. Two non-archival projects train on these data: one reports a process-twin control on v2~\cite{helali2026processtwin} and one a tile-level page classifier~\cite{leong2026docdet}; neither studies pixel-level ground truth.

\subsection{Ground-truth semantics and dataset shortcuts in forensics}
A network optimizes whatever signal its labels reward, which need not be the intended evidence~\cite{geirhos2020shortcut}. In forensics this has been shown for JPEG grid alignment~\cite{wong2025adcdnet,docqt2026}, for trivial artefacts of benchmark construction~\cite{smeu2025shortcuts,yermakov2026gend}, and for frequency bias~\cite{freqbias2025}. Inpainting studies report that whole-image re-decoding defeats splicing localizers while handing image-level detectors a global cue unrelated to the edit~\cite{mareen2024tgif,nebioglu2026inpx}, motivating generator-fingerprint localizers~\cite{giakoumoglou2026diffusionprint}; they also find that fine-tuned localizers acquire an object prior once masks are semantic~\cite{mareen2026tgif2}, and that small edits remain the hardest case~\cite{pandolfini2025localized,cai2026zooming}. Closest to our question, Shang et al.~\cite{shang2026maskspixels} argue that object-shaped masks label many unchanged pixels and miss real edits, so mask-only scores can rank detectors wrongly; on natural images, audits likewise question what pixel agreement measures~\cite{aoudi2026beyond} and separate the shifts behind poor generalization~\cite{giftbench2026}. In segmentation, box labels are a well-known form of weak supervision that must be refined before they approximate object extent~\cite{dai2015boxsup,tian2021boxinst}, and metric choice should follow the structure of the target~\cite{maierhein2024metrics}. We examine the same issue for box-annotated document forgeries, where the box is also the paste-back region.

\subsection{Detection versus segmentation for small regions}
Manipulation has been localized with region-proposal detectors since RGB-N~\cite{zhou2018rgbn}, and single-stage detectors~\cite{redmon2016yolo}, now available in maintained form as YOLO11~\cite{jocher2024yolo11}, predict boxes directly and are scored with COCO-style average precision~\cite{lin2014coco}. When the forensic label is a field box, a detector matches the annotation geometry by construction, whereas encoder--decoder segmenters such as U-Net and DeepLabV3+~\cite{ronneberger2015unet,chen2018deeplabv3plus} must spread a box label over pixels that may be unchanged. Edited fields in receipts are also small relative to the page; small objects are a recognized weakness of detectors, especially after downsampling~\cite{cheng2023smallobject}, and slicing the image into overlapping tiles at native resolution is a standard remedy~\cite{akyon2022sahi}. Multi-task models that predict boxes and masks jointly~\cite{he2017maskrcnn}, and recent forgery models that couple image-level detection with pixel localization~\cite{guangjian2026defakerone}, suggest that box and pixel supervision can be combined. To our knowledge, no prior study compares a box detector and a segmenter on the same AI-inpainted document benchmark while varying the semantics of the ground truth used for training and scoring.
