\section{Dataset and Audit}
\label{sec:data}
\textbf{AIForge-Doc v1}~\cite{wu2026aiforgedoc} contains 4{,}061 forged PNG images (3{,}249 train / 812 test) derived from CORD, WildReceipt, SROIE and XFUND; receipts form 89.7\,\% and forms 10.3\,\%. Each image has one edited field, produced by Gemini~2.5 Flash Image (89.6\,\%) or Ideogram~v2 Edit (10.4\,\%), and a binary mask. We recomputed all statistics from the files.

\textbf{Mask semantics.} All masks are single axis-aligned rectangles. With $D=\max_c|I^{f}_c-I^{a}_c|$ the per-pixel difference between a forgery $I^f$ and its authentic source $I^a$ (3{,}247 of 3{,}249 training pairs verified by perceptual hash and pixel difference), the median fraction of box pixels with $D>8$ is 0.49 (IQR 0.37--0.62), and 33.5\,\% of pairs also change pixels outside the box (Gemini 35.9\,\%, Ideogram 11.9\,\%), typically by re-rendering the whole text line. Half of the Gemini outputs carry a global $\pm$1--3 re-encoding difference that disappears at $\varepsilon\ge4$.

\textbf{Scale.} The median edited area is 0.98\,\% of the image (50.7\,\% of images below 1\,\%); the median box height is 33\,px while images span 0.03--35\,MP, so all models run at native resolution with tiling.

\textbf{The marker residue.} For Gemini, the dataset paper draws a 3-px bright-green rectangle on the context crop to mark the edit region~\cite{wu2026aiforgedoc} (Sec.~3.4 there). A saturated-green rule ($G-\max(R,B)>50$, $G>110$) restricted to a 6-px ring around the box and absent from the authentic image finds residue of this line ($\ge$20 px) in 83.5\,\% of the 2{,}466 Gemini and 1.4\,\% of the 294 Ideogram forgeries of the training split (no false alarm on 600 authentic images). Test forgeries mostly lack a paired authentic image, so for subset analyses only we label a test image as \emph{with residue} if any saturated-green pixel lies in the 6-px box ring (86\,\% of Gemini, 2\,\% of Ideogram forgeries over the whole dataset); model inputs are never altered by this label. Marker presence and generator are therefore strongly confounded (Cram\'er's $V=0.61$), more than generator and source ($V=0.43$; WildReceipt has no Ideogram edit).

\textbf{Splits and negatives.} The official test split is untouched. Training images are split by source-document groups into 2{,}762 train and 487 validation images with no group overlap; tool, source, area and marker distributions do not differ across the three splits ($\chi^2$ $p\ge0.30$). Of the test forgeries 8.9\,\% share a source document with training; we report the 740 leakage-free images separately. The released test ``authentic'' folder is not the mirror of the test forgeries (86 matches), so image-level detection is evaluated on validation pairs only. Fourteen degenerate training pairs (forged $\approx$ authentic, or all changes outside the box) are excluded from training (2{,}748 images remain). The dataset is gated and licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; beyond the examples shown in figures, we redistribute identifiers and per-image statistics only, not images or masks.
