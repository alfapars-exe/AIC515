
\subsection{External test on AIForge-Doc v2 (GPT-Image-2)}
\label{sec:res-v2}
AIForge-Doc~v2~\cite{wu2026aiforgedocv2} regenerates the v1 forgery specifications with GPT-Image-2 and pastes back only the edited box, so its forgeries differ from their authentic source only inside the box and carry no marker residue: no pixel outside the box differs in any of the 3{,}066 pairs, and our colour rule fires on a single green-tinted receipt, inside its box. We applied every model trained on v1 without retraining or recalibration ($\tau^*$ and image thresholds from v1 validation). Because v2 reuses v1's source documents, we fixed before scoring a leakage-free primary set: the 567 v2 pairs whose v1 counterpart is in the v1 test split and whose source document never appears in v1 training (Table~\ref{tab:v2}). Image-level AUROC uses the paired authentic source of every v2 forgery.

The box U-Net, 0.950 test AUPRC on v1, falls to $0.520{\pm}0.018$ (three seeds) and to 0.717 image AUROC; the tiled detector keeps $0.887{\pm}0.006$ and 0.919. Comparing the \emph{same} specification on v1 and v2 separates the generator change from the loss of the marker: on specifications whose v1 image carried the residue the box U-Net loses 0.49 per-image AP, on residue-free ones 0.17, and the box U-Net retrained on marker-repaired images loses nothing on residue-free specifications ($+0.003$, 95\,\% CI $[-0.06,0.06]$; one seed) and reaches 0.712 AUPRC overall. The detector loses only 0.03 on residue-free specifications. The marker-repaired pixel and glyph models are also the best pixel and glyph localizers on v2 (\GTp{} 0.430 vs.\ $0.246$; \GTg{} 0.446 vs.\ $0.304$). On a marker-free benchmark from a third generator, the box U-Net's v1 advantage over the repaired model is thus reversed, and most of what it loses is tied to the marker rather than to the generator. The ground-truth effect persists: every model scores 0.13--0.43 under \GTp{} on v2, and the detector's \GTp{} AUPRC (0.354) remains far below its \GTb{} AUPRC. On all 3{,}066 v2 pairs the detector (seed 42) reaches 0.912 image AUROC, 0.915 on the primary set and 0.911 on pairs whose source document was seen in v1 training, so document overlap barely matters; the dataset authors report 0.599 for zero-shot TruFor and 0.585 for DocTamper, a reference rather than a like-for-like comparison, since our detector was trained on v1.
